How this site handles inquiries

Are several fixtures slow, or does a drain keep backing up?

A slow sink, gurgling floor drain, or recurring sewer backup can have different causes, from a local blockage to roots, deposits, or a damaged section of pipe. For a drain-cleaning project in Stratford, note which fixtures are affected, when the symptoms occur, and whether the problem returns after clearing. Snaking can open a blockage, while hydro-jetting cleans pipe walls and camera inspection can help explain repeat clogs. Ask what method suits the pipe and how findings will be documented.
